Talk it out

Teachers who listen to what she actually says

Tap the microphone and just tell them. “I read Daddy the Goodnight Leelanau book” gets a real answer, a question about the story, and a big button to put it on her shelf. By default her voice is turned into words right on the device.

A bookshelf that grows

Every finished book flies onto her shelf. At 10, 25, 50 and 100 books the library itself grows — a reading nook, a grand library, a book dragon.

Hints, never red X's

Wrong answers get a gentle hint, a simpler step, or another way to see it. Questions get trickier as she's ready and easier when she isn't.

Grown-ups see it all

A parent studio with every teacher conversation word for word, progress on real standards, a portfolio, and reports for the family.

A school that grows with her

What she learns changes the world she walks around in

Plant beans and the greenhouse opens. Learn about space and a telescope appears. Finish Digit's missions and a rocket gets built, stage by stage.

Meet the teachers

Professor Hoot

Reading & storytelling

Warm, curious, and wildly enthusiastic about books. Chats about every story before it goes on the shelf.

Digit

Math

Playful, precise, and delighted by puzzles. Rescues moon rocks by counting, adding and sharing.

Nova

Science

Adventurous and always asking “why?” Predicts, tests and wonders out loud about what sinks and what floats.
